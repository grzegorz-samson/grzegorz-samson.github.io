Greggular

Native JUCE backend. Two Web interfaces.

Greggular connects a native JUCE plugin to two Web interfaces: the Premiere audiovisual installation and the Concepts granular instrument.

Grzegorz Samson Feliks Nowowiejski Academy of Music in Bydgoszcz, Poland
Try the showroom ↓

Research question

How can immersive interaction and direct instrument control support creative sound shaping?

FOUNDATION

The native plugin as the foundation

The JUCE VST3/Standalone plugin runs granular DSP and multichannel audio. Its native window, Premiere and Concepts provide interfaces to the same backend.

02 · Two contexts of use

Two Web interfaces

A

INSTALLATION MODE · WEB

Audiovisual environment

Water scene · installation interface render

Premiere brings conversational sound creation and prepared sets into an immersive audiovisual scene.

  • Water responding to gestures and audio
  • Optional camera reflection and motion
  • Conversation · Sage · Showcase
  • Presets and visual controls
B

INSTRUMENT MODE · GREGGULAR CONCEPTS

Virtual granular instrument

Greggular Concepts · instrument prototype screenshot

Concepts is a virtual instrument prototype: sound objects share granular centres, with contextual controls and MIDI.

  • Objects and shared granular centres
  • Waveform, range, gain and mute/solo
  • MIDI and reference pitch
  • Preset and sample library
