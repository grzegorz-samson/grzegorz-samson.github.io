Research idea

Generative technology as creative material

The project explores the creative and responsible use of new generative technologies in artistic workflows. Generated sound is not treated as a finished result, but as source material that can be selected, transformed and shaped by the artist in real time.

Generate

Create new source material or import existing recordings.

Transform

Shape the material through detailed real-time granular processing.

Perform

Combine, group and route evolving structures as an interactive instrument.
