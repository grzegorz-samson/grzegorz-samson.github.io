Greggular

A granular synthesis plugin. Three ways to create.

Developed from the research project “From Text Wishes to Sound”, presented at ADC25. Greggular supports work with sound as a plugin, an audiovisual installation, or a virtual instrument.

Interface and practice

One sound engine, different ways to interact

Greggular began as a granular synthesis plugin. Adding a Web UI made it possible to adapt the way people interact with it: a conversation with an installation, or direct performance on an instrument. The sound material and synthesis engine are shared; the gestures and the participant’s role change.

Sound installation

Interactive Installation: Wishing Well

Wishing Well invites visitors into a conversation with a sound installation. Their words and generated or selected sounds shape a granular soundscape. A water visualization adds a sense of immersion, while an optional camera mode can bring the visitor’s reflection into the image.

The installation was presented on 10 October 2026 during the celebration of 20 years of Sound Engineering at The Feliks Nowowiejski Academy of Music in Bydgoszcz, Poland.

Instrument and collaboration

GReggular Concepts

Concepts groups sound objects into granular centres for touch and MIDI performance. A shared LAN session lets participants join from their phones and shape the same scene together. The plugin on the host computer generates the audio.

The host shares an invite link or QR code and assigns access to selected sound groups. Several phones become control surfaces for one shared instrument.
