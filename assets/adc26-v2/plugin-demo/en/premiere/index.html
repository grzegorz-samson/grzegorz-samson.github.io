Audio emitters

Top is front; bottom is rear. Sparse grains have individual emitters, while dense clouds combine into channel sources. LFE sits in the middle. This local choice does not change audio routing.

Dots show possible emitter positions, not grains being played. This preview does not start audio or waves and is not saved.

Emitter count and audio waves

Set X/Y spread and placement above. Slow drift gradually moves emitters within each channel and only affects visuals. The combined emitter limit is 16; density can reduce the count, which recovers after 2 seconds of lower load. Size scales wave radius and strength scales audio sensitivity. The visual budget is 12 pulses/s, so intervals may lengthen with many sources.

Cloud size limits audio voices, not waves. Emitters use the nominal grain duration. A wave remains on the water and fades after emission ends.
